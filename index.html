About Weather Data

Weather information comes from the National Weather Service (NWS).

Temperatures may sometimes be different because:

  • Forecasts are predictions, not exact measurements.
  • Weather can change quickly.
  • The nearest weather station may be a few miles away.

Current temperatures use nearby NWS weather stations. Forecasts are updated regularly.
